# Fidelity

The emulator is a diagram that works. This phase makes it a convincing device:

1. **Fold by dragging the device**, not only by the pose bar — grab a leaf and
   swing it, snapping to the nearest pose, with the hinge angle live.
2. **The outer camera and Dynamic Island** drawn in place on the outer display,
   and **system bars** drawn vertically on its trailing edge, so examples can see
   what they must avoid (HIG, "Vertical controls").
3. **The inner camera** as an occlusion an example can switch on.
4. **Proportions** from measurement rather than eye, once there is something to
   measure (phase 0, *Deliberately open*).

**Done when.** Someone who has held a Duo, shown the explorer beside it, says
the poses and proportions look right — and can fold it by dragging.

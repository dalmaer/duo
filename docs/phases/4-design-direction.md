# Design direction

The first build is a three-column "lab bench": catalog, device, explanation. It
works; it is not necessarily right. Three directions were drawn side by side on
the project's isocan canvas (`.isocan/project.json`), each as a home screen, an
example screen and a phone screen, with the sources in
[docs/design/](../design/README.md):

- **A · Lab bench** — the current tool, refined: dense, precise, HIG citations inline.
- **B · Gallery wall** — a wall of live device cards by category; click to focus.
- **C · Storyboard** — one example at a time, all six poses at once as a filmstrip.

**Done when.** One direction is chosen, recorded here with the date and why,
and the live site matches its three screens.

**Deliberately open.** The choice itself — it is the owner's, not the agent's.

**The designer's recommendation (2026-09-30), not yet a decision.** Build
*Storyboard* as the spine — all six poses of one example at a glance — with a
frame expanding into *Lab bench* to play it, *Lab bench*'s pose matrix as a
secondary "Compare" view, and *Gallery wall*'s live cards only as the front
door. Full reasoning in [docs/design/README.md](../design/README.md).

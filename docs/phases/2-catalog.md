# The catalog

The examples, grouped the way people will look for them: **Music, Games,
Retro, Productivity, Learning, Patterns**. Each is drawn from something someone
built or posted in the weeks after the Duo launched, and credits them. The list
and where each came from is [docs/research/sources.md](../research/sources.md).

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Music | Cajón · Crate Sampler |
| Games | Battleships · Hinge Guess |
| Retro | Pocket Console · Duo-Man · Critterdex |
| Productivity | E-ink Reader · Sketchbook |
| Learning | Fold Cards |
| Patterns | List and Detail · Watch Above, Play Below |

**Done when.** Every example says what it does in each of the six poses, does
it, keeps its state through every change of pose, and has been played through
by a person in each pose it names as special.

**Deliberately open.**

- *Trademarks.* The viral red field guide and the green-screen handheld are
  clearly inspired by famous products. The examples are original — names,
  creatures, art — and should stay that way; a takedown would cost more than
  the resemblance is worth.
- *How many is enough.* Twelve covers every category with more than a token.
  New ones come through issues labelled `example`, not by default.

In table pose★ best

The bottom half is the plate and the fold is its top edge: slap near the hinge, bass lower down. The top half shows the instrument and the play-along lane.

Principle

Table pose puts content you watch on top and controls you touch on the stable bottom half — the destination Apple gives for at-a-distance content and tappable controls. HIG §6 · Destination follows purpose ↗

Also: keep controls off the crease §5 ↗ · size classes drive layout, not the pose §1 ↗

Pose sheet
ClosedA single pad with the box as a strip above it. Playable, but it is waiting for you to open it.
LandscapeTwo thumbs: left hand on the left, right hand on the right, like Ritmo's phone layout.
OpenThe box on the left page, the plate on the right. Everything works; it just isn't how you'd hold one.
PortraitThe same split as table pose, lying flat.
BookBox left, plate right — handy for watching someone else play.
